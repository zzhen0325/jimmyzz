Adapted for Jimmy ZZ: removed companion planet and invisible distortion shell,
reduced polygon count and texture resolution, converted legacy specular/glossiness
materials for Three.js, and adjusted scale, orientation and lighting for the homepage.
The outer rings are replaced by a texture-free procedural ring; additional light
layers are removed, with a single pale-gold light layer and local procedural glow.
The obsolete black outer masks are removed. The retained light layer and horizon
use their original geometry resolution; the procedural ring uses 512 segments.
The retained light shell is hidden at runtime and replaced with a smooth,
procedural lensed light path to preserve the central distortion without black masks.
